# 🎓 AI Study Companion

> A personalized learning workspace that helps learners study, practice,
> understand their progress, and identify what to learn next.

AI Study Companion is designed as a learning workspace rather than a
traditional chatbot. It brings study materials, AI-assisted learning,
assessments, concept mastery, progress analysis, and recommendations into
one continuous learning experience.

---

## ✨ Overview

Learning usually involves switching between notes, PDFs, practice questions,
progress trackers, and different AI tools.

AI Study Companion brings these activities together into a single workspace.

The application follows a continuous learning cycle:

```text
Space
  ↓
Project
  ↓
Learning Materials
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
AI Tutor
  ↓
Assessment
  ↓
Concept Mastery
  ↓
Growth Analysis
  ↓
Next Recommendation
  ↓
Continue Learning
